First consumer

soltempo: Solana DeFi yield
account for Tempo merchants.

The problem

  • Tempo merchants earn 0% on operating USDC
  • Solana has the deepest stablecoin DeFi (Kamino, Marginfi, Drift)
  • No primitive bridges merchant payments → DeFi yield atomically

The flow

  • Merchant USDC on Tempo → Buffer.sol
  • Above-buffer auto-bridges via CCIP → Solana vault → Kamino
  • Payouts pull back; each settlement = on-chain Receipt PDA

First narrow product proving the cross-VM thesis end-to-end. Deposit, earn, withdraw — atomically.

⏱ 1:17–1:39

The first consumer: soltempo. Tempo merchants today earn zero on operating balances. Soltempo bridges idle USDC to Kamino on Solana for yield, pulls back on demand for payouts. Each settlement is an on-chain Receipt PDA referencing the Tempo origin. One narrow merchant pain, one focused product.
